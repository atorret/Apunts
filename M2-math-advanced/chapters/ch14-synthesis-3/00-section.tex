\section{Synthesis III: three derivatives, three invariants}\label{sec:synth3}
% =====================================================================

\begin{synthesis}[what this section does]
Three different ``derivatives'' have been built: the exterior derivative $\dd$ (Section~\ref{sec:dform}), the Lie derivative $\Lie_X$ (Section~\ref{sec:lie}) and the covariant derivative $\nabla$ (Section~\ref{sec:conn}). % REWRITTEN: source labels (A), (B) removed.
They were introduced
separately; here they are compared, the three definitions of divergence are shown to agree, curvature is set against $\dd\dd=0$, and (in Section~\ref{sec:noethersynth} of Part~\ref{part:physics}, once Noether's theorem is available) the conservation laws of mechanics are matched with the Killing fields of general relativity.
\end{synthesis}
