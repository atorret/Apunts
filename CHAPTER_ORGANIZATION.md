The reading order that takes each document from its entry level to its target level. This is the only syllabus in the repository; [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) links here and does not repeat it.

- **Present** chapters are existing material, moved unchanged; the path is the chapter folder. Their prerequisites are the ones printed in the PDF (author-declared dependencies plus every earlier chapter the text refers back to).
- **TODO** chapters are named steps with no content yet (`TODO(DOC/chNN)`). Naming them is organization; no chapter body has been written. Their prerequisites are planned.
- Levels: basic, undergraduate, graduate, postdoc/research. A row gives the level that chapter reaches on the way to the target.
- Generated by `tools/gen_docs.py` from `tools/structure_config.py`; edit the config, not this file.
